Building & the map

How do I know where I can build a base?

Check the NHC map for designated zones and player-marked bases before choosing a spot. Some players also mark their base boundaries with wool pillars or similar markers.

How do I see zones and bases on the map?

Click the three-line menu in the top-left corner of the map, then enable the relevant layers under Markers. Bases use a Minecraft bed icon. You may need to zoom in to see them; hover over a marker to read the name the player has added.

What are the main zones, and what do they mean?

Zoom in near spawn to see three smaller zones:

  • Blue — Spawn Valley: a place to build a starter base.
  • Red — Shopping: reserved for shops. Do not build bases here.
  • Cyan — Public trading hall: the community villager trading area.

Zoom out to see the larger zones:

  • Blue — Industrial area: reserved for industrial redstone farms.
  • Red — Reset zone: do not build here. This area is reset with each update so new biomes can generate and resources can be replenished.
  • Yellow — Resource area: northwest of spawn, reserved for mining sand, terracotta, and coral. This area is also reset when necessary.
How can I mark my base on the map?

Place a sign with [base] on the first line and your base name on the second:

[base]
House Name
How can I mark my shop on the map?

Place a sign with [store] on the first line, followed by your shop name on the remaining lines:

[store]
Shop Name

Trading hall & the End

Where is the trading hall?

The center of the trading hall area is at X: -65, Y: 81, Z: -550.

How does the trading hall work?

Villagers are grouped into separate buildings by profession. The buildings are still being finished, but each villager type is available.

  • Farmers: X: -94, Y: 84, Z: -563. This building has a free-to-use pumpkin and melon farm collection area. Use these crops to trade for emeralds.
  • Librarians and clerics: X: -23, Y: 83, Z: -579. Free curing supplies are to the left of the door. Contact Azriellah if they need refilling.

All villagers have a zombie conversion setup. Flip the lever in front of the villagers you want to convert and cure, then flip it back up to access them again.

Both buildings also have emerald barrels you may take from. Please contribute when you can by spending time AFK at the pumpkin and melon farm or donating emeralds. Be considerate of other players.

Where is the End portal?

On the Nether roof, head directly west of spawn to X: -195, Z: 0. Look for a Nether portal with elytra wings on top. Go through it, then head toward the middle of the giant chasm at Trikki’s base. Cross the bridge to reach the End portal.

Can I use the farms in the End?

The Enderman XP farm is the only public farm in the End. All other farms are private, so do not take items from them without permission.

Where is the public Enderman XP farm?

From End spawn, follow the colorful tunnel to the elevator drop. Drop down, then follow the bridge to the public Enderman XP farm.

Can I use the elytra at End spawn?

Yes! Members provide loaner elytra and rockets so you can find an elytra of your own. Repair the borrowed elytra at the Enderman XP farm and return it afterward. If it goes missing, let members know in #members-chat so it can be replaced.

Can I use items left around the trading hall or Enderman XP farm?

Only use items that are clearly marked or provided for public use, such as the library’s curing supplies or the XP farm’s anvils and free Mending books. Other items may belong to players who left them behind. Please leave those alone.

Nether portals

How do I make a portal on the Nether roof?

Use a Nether portal calculator to work out the matching Overworld and Nether coordinates. Build both portals, then enter from the Nether side first to link them.

Why does my portal need to be 10 blocks above the Nether roof?

aiden_cole is building this season’s Nether hub, and the design needs space underneath the portals until they can be moved into their final positions. Raise your portal 10 blocks if it is within roughly 250 blocks of spawn. Portals outside that radius do not need to be raised.

Can I still decorate my portal?

Temporary decorations are welcome. Contact aiden_cole before adding permanent decorations.

Shopping

How do I find an item’s price?

Each shop displays prices differently. Look for renamed items in stock chests, such as an emerald named “1 diamond per stack.” Some shops use named shulker boxes to show the price of the contents, while others use signs.

What currency do shops use?

Diamonds, unless the shop’s pricing says otherwise. Each shop owner sets their own prices.

How do I find out which shops sell what?

At X: 165, Y: 66, Z: -422, you’ll find a map and books listing the shops in each district and what they sell. There is also a book where you can request items that are not currently sold. You can also browse shop adverts in Discord’s #shops-adverts-s5 channel.

How can I claim a shop plot?

Each district has its own governor and claiming process. In most districts, write your username and pay the required diamonds into a chest at the district’s central building. Some districts place their claiming chests elsewhere. Contact the governor if you need help.

What are the shopping districts, and who are their governors?
  • Market Square: Doomdots
  • Copperworks District: Mohobie
  • Emerald Keep: greengoose07
  • Pillarstone Plaza: DeucesMyGooses
  • Skyline District: DarkDuckthegreat
  • Overgrown District: Nickoo3
What theme should I use for my shop?

Follow the theme of the district you choose:

  • Market Square: small market stalls and shops, with flexible theming.
  • Copperworks District: steampunk, copper, and machinery.
  • Emerald Keep: medieval, weathered stone and rubble, market stalls, and carts.
  • Pillarstone Plaza: Moroccan-inspired builds, colorful fabrics, desert themes, stalls, and caravans.
  • Skyline District: modern buildings, skyscrapers, and European city architecture. Older and newer architectural styles can be mixed.
  • Overgrown District: plants, vines, moss, and nature reclaiming buildings.
Who is the President of Shopping, and what do they do?

CatboyQsco is the President of Shopping. They oversee the shopping area and introduce changes to help improve it.

Where can I advertise my shop?

Create a thread in Discord’s #shops-adverts-s5 channel. Write it in your own style, but include what you sell and where to find your shop. Members sometimes share these adverts in #members-chat too.

Community & events

Where can I build a minigame?

There are two minigame districts with no set theme: one near shopping and another farther out. The first district is quite full, so look for an available space or choose another suitable location on the server. DarkDuckthegreat is working on connecting these areas from the first minigame district.

Where can I post instructions for my minigame?

Create a thread in Discord’s #minigames channel to share instructions, rules, and any other information players need.

How do I organize an event?

Contact an event host or admin with your idea and the details. They can help you get it set up.

Server & support

What useful mods and data packs can I use on the server?

NHC has several features to make playing more convenient:

  • /unplug leaves a bot in your place so you can stay AFK without keeping Minecraft open.
  • /trigger formatname opens a menu for customizing item names and adding lore.
  • /trigger if_invisible makes an item frame invisible.
  • Sit: right-click an upward-facing stair, bottom slab, or carpet with an empty hand to sit down, just as you would with a cushion.
  • Recipe changes: additions include shapeless crafting, craftable red sand, stripping wood in a stonecutter, and smelting diorite into calcite in a blast furnace.
Who should I contact about server issues?

Contact Mohobie for most technical issues. You can also message any admin, who can pass the issue to the technical team.

What can I do if my connection is poor?

Try an alternative server address:

  • usw3.pixelbays.com:27000
  • global.nhc-smp.com

You can also try restarting your computer and router. If the problem continues, contact Mohobie or another admin for help.

What version is the server running?

The server is running 26.2, with plans to update to the latest version. Updates usually happen once the core mods and plugins support the new version.

What difficulty is the server set to?

The server is set to Hard difficulty.

What do the numbers next to our names in the player list mean?

They show the number of hours each player has spent on the server since it started on December 5, 2025.

Do I need any mods to join?

No mods are required. However, Simple Voice Chat is highly recommended so you can join in with other members. You do not have to speak—being able to hear others is fine!

Are TNT dupers allowed?

Yes, for simple farms. Ask an admin before using TNT dupers for large excavations, as big machines can cause lag and accidents.

How can I request a custom model or audio?

Open a ticket using YAGPDBxyz’s /ticket open command in Discord’s #bot-commands channel.
