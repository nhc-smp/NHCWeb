SERVER RULES

  1. BE WHOLESOME
    • No adult/NSFW content or discussions.
    • Keep swearing to an absolute minimum. (text and voice chat)
    • Discussions about mature or controversial subjects like Religion, Sexuality, Politics, etc should not be discussed within discord, game chat or voice chat. All forms of communication should remain appropriate for all ages(1-999).
    • Brief, non-explicit references to people, identities, relationships, or religions are welcome as long as they remain appropriate for all ages.
    • Treat others the way you wish to be treated.
  2. RESPECT OTHERS

    No harassment, bullying, hate speech, or discriminatory remarks. Do not target or shame others over sexuality, gender identity, pronouns, race, religion, disability, or personal beliefs. If someone sets a boundary or staff ask a topic to stop, move on.

  3. NO GRIEFING
    • Do not destroy, deface, or steal from others' builds or storages.
    • Do not leave a mess in the terrain of active areas.
    • Do not intentionally cause server lag.
  4. NO CHEATING

    This includes, but is not limited to: hacking, general item duplication (duping) and exploiting game mechanics. There is a zero tolerance policy for cheating.

    • Use of X-ray, or similar, texture packs is not allowed.
    • Duping of Falling Blocks is not allowed.
    • Use of “Freecam” is allowed if it’s not used to gain an unfair advantage.
    • Duping TNT is allowed if your farm requires it.
    • Duping rails, carpets and string is allowed if suitable item collection is in place.
    • Play minigames as intended and do not tamper with them.
  5. BE CONSIDERATE
    • When using voice chats, be mindful of microphone feedback/echo.
    • Do not kill people for no reason.
    • All farms and redstone contraptions must have an on/off switch.
    • Strive to improve the visual appeal of those ugly farms.
    • Spawn proof the Nether Roof.
  6. TAKE IT EASY!
    • Pranks must be in good taste and cleaned up afterwards.
    • Wars between players should be understood as purely role-play.
    • Respect that people may not wish to participate.
    • Advertising Discord or Minecraft servers is forbidden.
  7. KEEP IT COMMUNITY-APPROPRIATE

    This is primarily a Minecraft community. Keep off-topic discussion reasonable. Repeated discussion of controversial, explicit, or overly personal topics may be redirected by staff. If a conversation becomes heated, take it to staff instead of arguing in public.

  8. DO NOT BREAK DISCORD’S TOS
    • Follow Discord Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

We intend to imitate HermitCraft's fun, family friendly community.

Report issues to @Admin or @Community Mod

Please change your Discord "Server Profile" nickname to match your Minecraft username!

By joining and participating in our Minecraft server and associated Discord server, you agree to abide by these rules and contribute positively to our community.
